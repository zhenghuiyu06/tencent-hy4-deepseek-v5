THE MODEL, EXPLAINED

What is Hy4 Preview?

Hy4 Preview is a Mixture-of-Experts language model developed by Tencent's Hunyuan team. It contains 770 billion parameters in total while activating approximately 49 billion for each token. Those numbers describe its architecture: a request does not use all 770 billion parameters, and size alone cannot guarantee a better answer.

The Hy4 Preview 1 million-token context window gives demanding tasks room for source code, documents, instructions, and intermediate work. Published API limits distinguish that window from a 960k maximum input and a 64k maximum output. A good workflow still selects relevant material, keeps versions clear, and checks what the model actually retrieved.

Hy4 Preview is still being refined. Tencent has disclosed that some complex tasks may involve longer reasoning and more self-checking than necessary.

        BUILT FOR PRODUCTIVITY

        Hy4 Preview across four demanding modes of work.

        Hy4 Preview training data was built with Tencent engineers, game developers, finance analysts, and research teams around tasks they actually ship. The Hy4 Preview examples below describe where to test it, not guaranteed outcomes.

        01Software engineering

        Stay oriented across the whole build.

        Hy4 Preview is intended for development work that spans more than a single answer. Give it a feature brief, relevant code, and the constraints of your project; then ask for a plan, an implementation, and a verification pass. Tencent also highlights improvements in front-end visual judgment and interaction quality. Review the resulting diff and run your own tests before accepting changes.

        02Office & analysis

        Turn messy context into finished work.

        When information is scattered across notes, reports, tables, and drafts, Hy4 Preview can help organize it into a usable document, spreadsheet plan, or presentation outline. Its long context is useful for comparing related sources, but a large upload is not a substitute for selecting current and relevant evidence. Ask it to identify assumptions and show which source supports each conclusion.

        03Game development

        From one prompt to a playable prototype.

        Tencent positions Hy4 Preview for turning a game idea into a playable prototype and refining it over several turns. A practical prompt specifies the engine, controls, win condition, visual direction, and what should be built first. Follow-up prompts can focus on one subsystem at a time, such as movement, level design, or feedback, rather than restarting the whole project.

        04Scientific research

        Coordinate experiments, not just answers.

        Hy4 Preview is also designed for difficult research questions in areas such as AI, molecular dynamics, condensed matter physics, and mathematics. Use it to map a problem, compare hypotheses, and propose checks that could disprove an answer. For research work, the strongest output is often a clear next experiment and a list of unresolved assumptions, not an unverified final claim.

        COMMON QUESTIONS

        Hy4 Preview, in plain terms.

        Is Hy4 Preview the finished Hy4 release?

        No. Tencent describes it as an early version with room for further pre-training and post-training. Treat its current behavior as something to evaluate on your own tasks.

        What does 49B active mean for Hy4 Preview?

        Hy4 Preview has 770B parameters in total, but its expert routing activates about 49B for each token. The active count and the full weight size answer different questions.

        Does the Hy4 Preview 1M context make every document useful?

        It is a capacity limit, not a recommendation to upload everything. Current, relevant, clearly labeled sources are easier to reason over than duplicate or contradictory files.

        Can I use Hy4 Preview for coding?

        Coding and longer engineering workflows are among Tencent’s stated priorities. Give the model a concrete project brief and validate its changes with your normal development tools.

        What do High and No Think change in Hy4 Preview?

        For Hy4 Preview, High asks for deeper reasoning on difficult tasks. No Think aims for a more direct response. The best choice depends on the task, and the resulting answer still needs review.

        How should I read the Hy4 Preview benchmark chart?

        The headline comparison comes from Tencent’s internal blind engineering test. It shows a modest advantage on that test; it does not prove that Hy4 Preview is best for every workload.
